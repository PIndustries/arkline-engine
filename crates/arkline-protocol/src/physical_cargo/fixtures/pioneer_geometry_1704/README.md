# Pioneer physical geometry candidate 1704

This exact opt-in registry is a geometric source configuration, not normal carrier,
flight, warp, mass, structural or operational admission. It does not reinterpret
legacy prototype units. Source 1703 and its collision fixtures remain unchanged.

The original final assembly manifest is preserved byte-for-byte and pins all 20
source/model/proof assets. Ten compact receipts are embedded and individually
checked against that manifest before the typed configuration is constructed.
The large Blender, GLB, full authoring and export-receipt files are not embedded
in the protocol binary. Their original paths are provenance identifiers, not
paths a consumer is permitted to open blindly. A consumer installing those
assets must verify the actual bytes with `validates_asset_bytes`.

The exact original cells are read from the ship counted-cell receipt and the
drone full-cell proof. Rational metre coordinates must convert exactly to integer
millimetres. Capacity is the sum of the individually floored cell volumes:
214,121,238 cm³ for the ship and 893,833 cm³ for the mining drone. The assembly
manifest, measurement receipt, source and GLB identity all remain bound together.
The entire source configuration has a separate stable SHA-256 identity.

The explicit new receiver contract uses 94 mm travel rather than the nominal
95 mm metadata preserved in old sources. No runtime selector is updated by this
registry, and no operating rates, material prices, densities or ratings are
inferred from geometric volume.
