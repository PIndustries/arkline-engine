# Selected physical bay inspection

This is a deliberately bounded connected mechanics inspection, not normal gameplay
admission, a new ship class limit, a released client build or an MMO scaling result.
The existing editor and normal 1703 inventory remain unchanged.

## Boundary

The 1704 source provider supplies the actual bay 0 Mining body's launch, service and
capture poses. The connected executor admits only an exact source-completeness
certificate and an untouched singleton physical-volume inspection world. Other
bays, reserve staging, other classes, unrelated host mechanisms and the ship itself
remain fixed or unavailable. Source pose interpolation is continuous and milestones
are acknowledged only after their endpoint is reached. Normal geometry lookup and
normal transport admission remain closed.

The complete current exported source is classified against the selected swept
motion domains. Local material and current source/export coverage must pass;
remote objects may be omitted from narrow-phase checks only with conservative
unreachability evidence and their certified fixed poses. The route handling proof
retains its explicit 10 micrometre contact boundary. It is not a claim of a
structural/load rating, zero-gap manufacturing certification, dynamic-world
collision acceptance or whole-fleet concurrency.

## Connected authority

`apps/server/src/physical_cargo/bay.rs` owns the selected source-timed executor.
`apps/verifier/src/physical_cargo/bay.rs` independently replays its typed events.
The world JSON, economic ledger envelope and SQLite transaction boundary are the
existing ones. Each motion decision and its required physical-volume events commit
atomically. The versioned bay binding enters a stricter ruleset 4 inspection mode;
raw accounting events cannot bypass a bound bay. Reopen checks replayed state and
freezes accepted motion and transfer rather than advancing offline.

One loop is: launch, reach the certified exterior endpoint, perform an authored
inspection mining cycle, approach the source service pose, open the donor and
receiver valves in order, transfer at the reached coupled barrier, close them in
reverse order, redeploy, then return through the capture route. The receiver's
contract is 94 mm and the drone inlet's is 250 mm. Source frame equality at each
route join is required; resetting to a canonical pose must not hide a teleport.

## Fixture inputs versus physical facts

The ship's conservative geometric storage is 214,121,238 cm³; the drone's is
893,833 cm³. Source receipts establish those empty-volume cells and exclusions.
They do not establish material density, battery size, safe payload mass, structural
strength or pressure ratings.

The mining target, actuator segment duration, extraction/manifold rates, resource
quantity and energy budget in these tests are explicit authored inspection inputs.
The fixed mining target and source muzzle/beam corridor must pass the same exact
source certificate. They are not evidence of unrestricted travel to normal-world
asteroids, existing gameplay power-system integration or final balance tuning.
Health, energy and volume changes in the inspection are authoritative, checked and
replayed; that distinction does not make its setup a packaged playable mining trip.

The current physical-volume policy reserves ship capacity for all carried drone
payload and pending extraction claims. A full hold therefore prevents additional
extraction reservations before already reserved cargo arrives. This is the existing
bounded inspection policy, not an implicit final rule for every future ship/fleet.

## Acceptance and subsequent gates

Exact test counts, source hashes and passed/not-run checks belong in dated receipts.
Required selected-bay checks include two connected cycles and final return,
intermediate poses, both valves/contact, volume conservation, energy/range gates,
hold/cancel/restart, stale commands, no-write invalid actions, due-time loss,
transaction rollback and rehashed adversarial replay. A measured full-hold core
boundary test does not imply hundreds of connected trips were executed.

After this bounded inspection passes, extend the same integrated candidate to the
other ready bays and inventory identities, real concurrent reload/return/service,
normal-world mining and power adapters, moving-ship/warp safety and the existing
renderer/controls. Preserve the normal-admission gate until those results exist.
Performance remains a separate measured release gate; the last verified predecessor
still exceeded the 100 ms heavy-tick budget. No source proof supplies an FPS or
end-to-end input-latency result.
