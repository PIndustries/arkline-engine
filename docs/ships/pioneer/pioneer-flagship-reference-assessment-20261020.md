## Measured cockpit correction

A **whole workstation translation of (−1,0,0) m**, including chair, joints, seat anchors, consoles and all screen anchors, gives seated eye `(33.22,1,3)` m. It preserves the existing screen/hand reach relationship; moving only the chair would leave controls about a metre farther away.

Revise the first bay proposal: **rear inner face X≈31.6**, supported floor X31.6..34.9, Z−6.8..5.8, with existing floor Y−0.32. The old X32.4 suggestion left only 0.3 m behind the translated wallplate at X32.7, less than the 0.6 m avatar collision diameter. X31.6 gives a nominal 1.1 m aft aisle before new details.

Read-only GLB axis rays at `(31.6,1,3)` hit the outer roof at Y4.9333, floor at Y−4.9967 and side boundaries at Z±7.4. Candidate rear-plane corners at Y−0.32/2.88 and Z−6.8/+5.8 remain inside the outer hull. The existing rear wall at X33.65 still blocks the forward view. Exact source triangles31368/31371 form its main face; at ceiling height Y2.88 the adjacent bevel triangle31425 is hit at X33.6606. A full-height cut must include the audited intersecting bevel pieces rather than assuming those two large triangles are sufficient.

The existing chair is wall-mounted: its plate at X33.70..33.77 sits just ahead of the old hull wall. Translation puts that plate at X32.70..32.77, so the deeper bay needs an explicit deck-fixed chair base/back-frame. Leaving the plate suspended in the new room would be physically inconsistent. Keep the new base out of the standing root and validate the remaining aft aisle and folded-pan sweep.

This is a proposed new fitted bay, not a recovered authored interior or engineering certification. It requires shared rear/side/ceiling boundaries, support/collision, connector termination/portal, source clipping and unchanged outer-envelope tests. Keep the existing port exit connection explicit. Detailed ray data and superseded/corrected candidates are in [fit JSON](pioneer-cockpit-fit-20261020.json).

## Recommended implementation order and ownership

1. **Complete and verify canonical continuity first.** Renderer/root owns same geometry, eye, instruments, glass treatment and lighting across docked/space/external views. Retain the same source hash and authority state through the transition. Compare actual views at the same body pose and look direction.
2. **Fit the cockpit and make exit legible.** One geometry owner owns protocol `pioneer_docked.rs` plus client `pioneer_docked_source.rs`: bay, complete workstation translation, source cut, connector and collision/support tests. Root/client UI owns clear boarding/exit guidance using the existing authority sequence. Verify walking behind the chair and around consoles, free look, near clip, both decks and complete boarding round trip.
3. **Give shared materials distinct physical responses and add a restrained finish.** A visual owner should map shared roles to the renderer before adding deck grip/plating seams, warm light wall panels, dark service ceiling cassettes, metallic structural/hatch hardware, nonemissive emerald trim, gasketed glazing and instrument housings. Reuse the same recipe across roles/decks/space. Deterministic metric-scale seams/grip are useful; random noise and many decorative mesh copies are not a substitute for construction detail. Record primitive/draw/light counts and actual CPU/GPU timings before widening detail.
4. **Make a separate room-program/boarding decision.** Reconcile D2-P1 versus actual bridge and lower EVA room versus upper boarding vestibule, then plan purpose-specific equipment footprints. Preserve installed room IDs and user layouts until a reviewed layout revision/migration exists. Proposed service endpoints can guide accessible housings and routing but do not grant simulation authority.
5. **Author one bounded moving hatch before broader mechanisms.** Protocol/server/client owner must define motion state, timing, interruption/power policy, travel pocket, swept occupancy and conservative blocked traversal until clear. Existing booleans cannot prove a safe animation. Crane/gear/cargo handling and general animation remain later work.
6. **Continue exterior fidelity as a measured source revision.** Work on large side/collar/underside forms before more small detail. Preserve hardpoints and current source identity until explicit export/material rebinding and matched front/top/side/quarter/bottom review. Do not bundle a new model export into an interior material pass.
