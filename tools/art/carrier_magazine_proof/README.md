# Source-bound carrier magazine fixture

This is the explicit stationary Stage/Launch fixture for 10 ready positions and
10 protected reserve cassettes. It does not admit a flyable hull or alter legacy
Pioneer assets/worlds. Normal geometry lookup remains unavailable.

`candidate_routes.py` owns exact translated roots for three-leg cassette exchange
and physical Launch. `build_provider_fixture.py` compiles those same roots into
the protocol fixture and native source partition. Only the selected cassette IDs
and owned machinery may be remapped/merged; other cassette identities never reset.

Checks cover all 50 same-side exchanges, all three actual drone meshes, all ten
Launch paths, source contacts/connectivity, static hardware, occupied peer bodies,
physical cassette permutation, opposite-side domains, and arbitrary-timing
Stage/other-berth Launch domains. Negative controls deliberately close a transfer
hatch and remove required load-path joints. These are geometric/contact proofs,
not mechanical strength, atmosphere, power, damage or travel certification.

One Deploy All command must pipeline the finite eligible roster using actual
resource/clearance checks. The geometry has no two-wave timer or global barrier.
Buffers are transit-only and add no stock capacity. Cross-side loading is absent.

Final candidate provenance and acceptance receipts are retained externally under
`pioneer-magazine-ready-20261026/`; failed earlier candidates remain evidence.
Recovery, ore service, turret composition, general-world bounds/admission and
native continuous twenty-drone operation remain subsequent integration gates.
